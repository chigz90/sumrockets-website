# sumrockets-website

The Sum Rockets website – https://www.sumrockets.co.uk – a landing page, Privacy Policy and Support.

Plain HTML and CSS, no build step, no frameworks, no third-party scripts, no analytics or cookies.
Nunito is self-hosted (`assets/fonts`, SIL Open Font License – see `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`).

- `index.html` – landing page (the App Store switch is the `APP_STORE_ID` line at the bottom)
- `privacy/` – Privacy Policy
- `support/` – Support and FAQ
- `LAUNCH.md` – launch-day steps

Preview locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000` then open http://localhost:8000
